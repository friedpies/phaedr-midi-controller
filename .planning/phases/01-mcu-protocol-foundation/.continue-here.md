<decisions_made>

- SERIAL → DEVICE_SERIAL rename in mcuProtocol.h/cpp: Teensy framework's wiring.h defines `#define SERIAL 0`, causing preprocessor collision. Renamed to DEVICE_SERIAL; byte values identical.
- LED_MAX_BRIGHTNESS = 180: Chosen for USB 500mA budget (24 LEDs × 14.1mA avg + 150mA Teensy ≈ 489mA, within limit).
- Noise threshold for Fader = 48: Proportional scaling of Potentiometer's ANALOG_NOISE=3 from 10-bit to 14-bit range.
- Knob CC reassignment (MCU-06): CC 14-15, 28-31, 118-119 → CC 16-23, applied during Plan 05 InputManager wiring.
</decisions_made>
